# 5I28, kits and FoldForge re-measured with the steady-forward protocol (H100, 2026-11-15)

Both sides of the [5I28 benchmark](../guides/BENCHMARK-5I28.md) re-measured with ONE timing protocol, to replace the "every forward after the first"
median of the [2026-11-11 kit record](5i28_anthropic_kits_20261111.md) and the 3-repeat FoldForge median of the
[2.5.9 record](5i28_foldforge_20261115_engine_259.md). Data: [data/5i28_steady_20261115.csv](data/5i28_steady_20261115.csv) (every cell: all forwards in order,
cold, second, the protocol value, min / max of the timed forwards, the old value and change, kit / FoldForge new and old, job, node, the jobs of this run
that overlapped it). This file is not edited afterwards.

## Protocol

- Per (model, n) cell: forward 1 (cold) and forward 2 are discarded, then 5 timed forwards; the value is their median. Why forward 2: several kits do
  extra work on the second call of a shape (the ESMFold2 kit's `ef2_opt._VerifiedCache` recomputes featurisation + ESM-C on call 2 to confirm its
  cache: +95% at n=1 here; AF3-JAX's second forward +152% at n=1). Five timed forwards so that one Python gen-2 GC pause (0.15-0.33 s) cannot move the median.
- `gc.collect()` outside the timed span before every timed forward: ESMFold2 (`esm_fwd_multi.py`) and RoseTTAFold3 (`fwdtimer.py`), whose harness
  times the forward itself. The other kits loop internally (seeds / items in one process) and FoldForge's `measured_forward` too: no GC control there,
  the median of 5 is the protection.
- Exceptions: Chai-1 n=16 has 4 timed forwards (each fold ~484 s of call time, 388 s of it ranking / writing after the timed span; 7 folds do not fit
  a 1 h job). ESMFold2, Boltz-2 and OpenDDE n=16 are out of memory again (as on 2026-11-11).
- Everything else as the protocol of the earlier records: n = 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16 chains of 5I28 (128 tokens each), real MSA (P0113359) + 4 distinct
  real templates (RoseTTAFold3 1 per chain, ESMFold2 none), 4 trunk passes, 200 steps, 5 samples, bf16, each kit's own forward-only timer span
  (listed in the 2026-11-11 kit record), FoldForge `model_seconds_warm` with compile + CUDA graphs (scope trunk).
- Harness changes (copies in the measurement directory, the repo harness untouched): Protenix family / OpenDDE seeds 0-6 (still one process per n),
  AF3-JAX / AF3-Torch inputs with `modelSeeds [0..6]` (otherwise byte-identical), OpenFold3 / RF3 `COPIES=7`, Chai-1 `FWDS=7` (n=16: 6), Boltz-2 7 copies
  per n; FoldForge `REPEATS=6` (cold + 6 warm; warm 1 dropped). The 2026-11-11 Boltz-2 markers listed every forward twice (the appended
  `pred_worker.log` repeats the PHASE lines); this run reads them once.

## Conditions

- NVIDIA H100 80GB HBM3, **node02 for every job**, one GPU per job, up to 6 jobs at the same time (6 chains), Slurm `-c 16 --hint=nomultithread`,
  pinned cores; other users' jobs shared the node (squeue snapshots per cell in the measurement directory). Training jobs ran on node01 at the same time.
- Kits: the same kit venvs / container as 2026-11-11 (versions listed there). FoldForge: engine 2.5.9 (pin `4f2f329`), scratch worktree environment,
  warm compile caches.
- Measurement directories: `~/miniworld-engine-scratch/bench5i28_1115_steady` (kits; `done_final/` markers, `logs/`, `probe/`, `analysis/`) and
  `~/miniworld-engine-scratch/bench5i28_ff/run_1115_259_steady` (FoldForge). Jobs 54084-54120 (FoldForge 54084-54089, 54101, 54107, 54114).

## Reading

- **The protocol changes little.** Outside Chai-1 every kit cell moved by at most a few percent (largest: RF3 n=8 -3.3%, ESMFold2 n=1 -4.5%,
  Protenix v1 n=8 +3.2%, Boltz-2 n=1 +2.5%, OpenDDE n=1 +2.6%), and every FoldForge cell by at most 4.3% (OpenDDE n=8 -4.3%, OpenFold3 n=2 -3.8%).
  The kit / FoldForge ratios move by at most ~0.1 outside Chai-1.
- **Chai-1 is 13-26% faster than in the 2026-11-11 record at every n, and that is not the protocol:** its second forward is not slower than the rest
  here (-1.5% to +0.1%). The 2026-11-11 Chai-1 cells ran on node01 (that record already noted Chai-1 8-24% slower that day on node01); this run is on
  node02. The Chai-1 kit / FoldForge ratio therefore drops from 12.62 / 5.84 / 5.04 / 3.70 / 2.73 / 2.49 to 10.13 / 5.07 / 3.86 / 2.72 / 2.10 / 1.87.
  Not re-checked here: a node01 run of the kit under this protocol.
- Second forward: AF3-JAX (+152% at n=1 down to +2%) and ESMFold2 (+95% to +4%) are the kits whose second call is slow; OpenFold3 +4% at n<=4; the
  others within +-3%. FoldForge's second forward is within +-1.3% except OpenFold3 n=2 (+4.1%).
- Spread of the 5 timed forwards is above 5% in a few cells (AF3-JAX n=1 7.6%, RF3 n=1 6.6%, ESMFold2 n=12 5.6%, OpenDDE n=1 8.3%, Boltz-2 n=4 21.6%:
  one 3.28 s forward among four 2.70-2.73 s); the median is not moved by one outlier.
